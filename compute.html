Is decentralized compute cheaper?

GPU rental price on decentralized networks against the same GPU at a centralized provider. Left of zero is cheaper, right is more expensive. Latest price per GPU and network. Prices are what each network lists (Akash: the average of its offers; io.net: its VM price), and "free" is how many were available to rent when the price was read: a low price you can't rent is not a low price.

What does one agent run cost?

What a benchmark's authors report spending on one run of each model, with the same harness for every model on that benchmark. All bars share one scale. Each benchmark has its own set of models: the ones its authors costed, at the time they ran it, so the scores can differ from the current leaderboard.
