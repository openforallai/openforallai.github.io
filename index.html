Is decentralized compute cheaper?

GPU rental price on decentralized networks against the same GPU at a centralized provider. Left of zero is cheaper, right is more expensive. Latest price per GPU and network. Prices are what each network lists (Akash: the average of its offers; io.net: its VM price), and "free" is how many were available to rent when the price was read: a low price you can't rent is not a low price.

How far behind are open models?

The best closed, best open and best Korean model on each benchmark, from the benchmarks labs most often report. Scores are independent (Epoch AI and public leaderboards) wherever they exist; a maker's own number is used only where none does, and is marked *. Makers' own numbers tend to run higher than independent ones (Anthropic reports 60.9% on Humanity's Last Exam for Claude Fable 5.1; the independent leaderboard measured 46.5%), so a gap that mixes the two flatters the self-reported side.
